# The Six-Vertex Wall — live candidate lemmas

**These are targets, not established theorems.** Each carries its route and falsifier from the audited bank.

## C-001 — Completion-to-Blockade Extraction
**Route:** MWIS/chordal route

**Candidate:** Convert a suitable minimal chordal completion or balanced clique-tree separator into a polynomial pure pair, restricted set, or bounded attachment quotient in the original graph.

**Falsifier:** A P6-free family whose useful completions have no polynomial original-graph pure structure.

## C-002 — Bounded Mixed-Type Theorem
**Route:** anchored P5 signatures

**Candidate:** Around an induced P5, persistent mixedness among exterior signatures yields P6 or a bounded pure quotient with EH pieces.

**Falsifier:** An unbounded realizable signature system with no P6 and no large pair.

## C-003 — Comb-to-Path Propagation
**Route:** property (*)

**Candidate:** Repeated uncontrolled comb-tooth mixedness yields co-P6, a polynomial homogeneous set, or a wide pure blockade.

**Falsifier:** A terminal-free comb with persistent mixedness.

## C-004 — P5/P5 Tooth Decomposition
**Route:** HJZ Lemma 1.14

**Candidate:** Partition each tooth into a P5-free residual and pure quotient with P5-free pattern, externally pure to other teeth.

**Falsifier:** A co-P6-free comb tooth for which every such partition fails.

## C-005 — P6 Tooth Quotient Lemma
**Route:** mixed-component quotient

**Candidate:** Construct a fine tooth blockade whose mixed components and quotient pattern lie in fixed EH classes.

**Falsifier:** A minimal comb with an uncontrolled component or quotient.

## C-006 — Alternating Block-Path Lemma
**Route:** wonderfulness

**Candidate:** A vertex mixing many anti-connected blocks with complete/sparse block pairs yields co-P6 or a large restricted/pure structure.

**Falsifier:** A wonderfulness-blockade survivor.

## C-007 — Anchor Synthesis
**Route:** wonderfulness transfer

**Candidate:** Mixed anti-connected blocks synthesize the opposite-anchor incidences needed for T-006, or already yield a terminal.

**Falsifier:** A survivor with neither anchors nor a terminal.

## C-008 — Minimal Comb Obstruction Census
**Route:** finite classification

**Candidate:** Classify all bounded two-/three-tooth configurations and lift them to a universal comb theorem.

**Falsifier:** A higher-rank survivor or failed symbolic lift.

## C-009 — Active Rectangle Purification
**Route:** local extremal

**Candidate:** Under T-006 and denied homogeneous sides, obtain polynomial pure subpairs or bounded quotient complexity.

**Falsifier:** A large survivor with no balanced pure pair.

## C-010 — Multi-Tooth Twin Synchronization
**Route:** global profiles

**Candidate:** Coordinatewise near-twin relations synchronize into wide joint-profile classes.

**Falsifier:** Exponentially many incompatible joint codes consistent with local laws.

## C-011 — Global Sparse Orientation Collapse
**Route:** directed tooth pattern

**Candidate:** Retain many teeth with one compatible sparse orientation, or obtain a terminal/P6.

**Falsifier:** A complex dense/sparse tooth pattern with no terminal.

## C-012 — Terminal Comb Theorem
**Route:** property (*)

**Candidate:** Every complement-side P6-free comb yields a polynomial homogeneous set, polynomial pure pair, full width-w/ell^2 pure refinement, or P6.

**Falsifier:** A terminal-free P6-free comb.

## C-013 — Polynomial Stable/Restricted Slice Extraction
**Route:** upstream comb wall

**Candidate:** Every tooth yields a polynomial stable/restricted slice or an immediate EH terminal, noncircularly.

**Falsifier:** A tooth resisting all structured extraction.

## C-014 — Direct P6 Polynomial Restriction Lemma
**Route:** Rodl bypass

**Candidate:** Every P6-free graph has an epsilon-restricted induced subgraph of size epsilon^d n.

**Falsifier:** A sequence violating every fixed d.

## C-015 — Dominator Rectangle Law
**Route:** cograph core

**Candidate:** Incomparable exterior classes around an anticomplete cograph split obey a generalized rectangle/crown law without stable cells.

**Falsifier:** A local union-node interaction outside all transition types.

## C-016 — Dominating-Cograph Entropy Collapse
**Route:** direct induction

**Candidate:** A small cograph dominator yields a large type, pure pair, restricted set, or induced P6.

**Falsifier:** A small dominator with resistant high-entropy exterior.

## C-017 — Terminal Weighted-Dominator Lemma
**Route:** mass route

**Candidate:** A dominating cograph with exterior mass gives a polynomial homogeneous set or reduces to a strictly simpler heavy module.

**Falsifier:** Adversarial mass with no homogeneous accumulation.

## C-018 — Cotree Mass Dichotomy
**Route:** terminal direct route

**Candidate:** At every cotree node, homogeneous mass, union-node crown control, join-node clique accumulation, or strict heavy-child reduction occurs.

**Falsifier:** A minimal weighted cotree counterexample.

## C-019 — Cotree Transition Classification
**Route:** finite-to-symbolic

**Candidate:** Classify all two-anchor/two-cell union/join transitions and prove composability.

**Falsifier:** A transition requiring unbounded context.

## C-020 — Potential-Maximal-Clique Pure Separator Lemma
**Route:** algorithmic route

**Candidate:** A balanced PMC in a P6-free graph yields a polynomial pure pair or restricted side in the original graph.

**Falsifier:** A separator whose attachments remain maximally mixed.

## C-021 — Viral P6 Lemma
**Route:** induced-copy route

**Candidate:** Few induced P6 copies force a polynomial restricted set.

**Falsifier:** Low-P6-density graphs with no restricted subset.

## C-022 — Auxiliary-H Wonderfulness Witness
**Route:** HJZ criterion 2

**Candidate:** Find |H|>=6 with H+ and H- containing co-P6 and {H,co-P6} having EH.

**Falsifier:** Exhaustive nonexistence or unresolved pair-EH.
